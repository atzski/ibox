Music in the Box

Your music, over your game.

Control YouTube Music, or any Windows music player, without leaving your game. One shortcut, and a glass panel slides over the action.

Install in iBoxStandalone app

In iBox: Settings › Plugins. Free and open source, Windows 10 and 11.

Try it.

The real panel, over a real game: click, search, open the lyrics. Alt+C closes and reopens it.

Demo: the library is an example, no music is played.

Mid-game

Alt+C on the keyboard, or View + Y on a controller. The panel takes over while it is open and hands control back as soon as it closes.

Glass that follows the game

It blurs what is behind it and takes on the colours of the cover art. It never shows up in your captures.

All your music

Playlists, Up next, lyrics and search in YouTube Music. Without an account, it controls Spotify, Deezer or a browser tab.

Music in the Box is not affiliated with YouTube or Google.
