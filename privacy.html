Privacy and legal notice

Privacy

What stays on your PC

  • Your library (games, folders, play time, collections, favorites), your settings, your profiles, your screenshots and your clips are stored on this PC only.
  • API keys (Steam, SteamGridDB, RetroAchievements, GIPHY) and the iNetbox session are kept in the Windows credential vault, never in plain text.
  • Your games are never sent anywhere, moved or modified. Game saves are only copied, on this PC, when you ask.
  • The problem report and the incident log are created on this PC; you decide whether to share them. iBox sends no usage statistics.

iNetbox (optional)

  • If you create an account, the iNetbox server keeps: your username, your email address, your display name, your password as an irreversible hash (never readable), your friend code, your friends, your blocks, your privacy choices and the profile items you choose to show.
  • Your online status and current game are shared only if you turned this on, and only with your friends: the status disappears after 90 seconds without news, the history of launched games after 7 days.
  • If you share your library, your game list is sent so your friends can see the games you have in common; turning sharing off erases it from the server.
  • Private and group messages are kept for 90 days, then erased. A game invite contains the game title and its console; it is deleted after 30 minutes.
  • Community: the images you share (8 MB at most) are visible to your friends, or to everyone if your profile is public. Only your 12 most recent are kept, with their reactions and replies; you can delete them at any time.
  • Remote Play: iNetbox only puts the two PCs in touch (session erased after 15 minutes of inactivity) and keeps your devices' public key. Picture, sound and controls go directly from PC to PC, encrypted, without passing through the server.
  • The server is hosted by Render and the database by Neon. Deleting your account (iNetbox › My account) erases all this data.

Services contacted

  • For covers and information: libretro-thumbnails and libretro-database (GitHub), GameTDB, LaunchBox Games Database, SteamGridDB (with your key) and iiDB. Only a game's name or ID is sent.
  • For manuals: ScreenScraper, Internet Archive and Steam. Only a game's name or identifier is sent.
  • For achievements: RetroAchievements (with your username and key) and the Steam Web API.
  • For your PC games: the Steam Web API, with your key and Steam ID, if you set it up.
  • iBox Platforms: emulators are downloaded, at your request, from their official sources (GitHub or the emulator's website). For Remote Play, the ViGEmBus virtual controller driver comes from GitHub, with your consent.
  • SmartConfig: EmuReady receives the game title to find settings shared by the community.
  • Remote Play: public STUN servers (Cloudflare, Google) help the two PCs find each other; they see your IP address, like any website.
  • Decoration and avatar images: SteamGridDB, GIPHY (with your key, only the search term is sent), Pokémon Showdown, GitHub and Riot Games (Data Dragon). Only images are downloaded.
  • iBox updates are checked on GitHub (atzski/ibox); no personal data is sent.
