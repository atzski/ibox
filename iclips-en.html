iClips

The studio for your game clips.

Keep the last seconds of your game without leaving it. Trim, go vertical, and drop the clip into Discord. Everything happens on your PC.

Install in iBoxStandalone app

In iBox: Settings › Plugins. Free and open source, Windows 10 and 11.

Try it.

The real Studio, with real game footage: open a clip, trim it, make it vertical. The editing is done for real, in your browser.

Demo: footage from the trailers on the games' Steam pages and official screenshots, property of their publishers. Nothing leaves your browser.

Mid-game

Alt+X or View + X: the panel keeps the last seconds, up to two minutes, names the clip and freezes a screenshot. It never shows up in your clips.

“Clip that!”

Say it, hold View + A, or let the end of the game keep the last moment. Windows recognises the phrase on your PC: nothing is sent.

Edit and share

Trim, join, make a GIF or a vertical cut for TikTok, Shorts and Reels. Drag the clip into Discord, or create a link that lasts 72 hours.

72-hour links go through Litterbox, a third-party host; everything else stays on your PC.
